## What the thread is about
Hand-built cluster: Kubernetes 1.10, Flannel (+ containerd CNI bridge config `10-containerd-net.conflist`), containerd as the CRI. kube-dns (pod 10.244.0.2) cannot list services: `Get https://10.96.0.1:443/api/v1/services: dial tcp 10.96.0.1:443: getsockopt: no route to host`; probes time out and kube-dns is killed. The host `ip route` has the pod routes (flannel.1, cni0) and no route for the Service CIDR (normal: Service IPs are rewritten by kube-proxy, not routed). kube-proxy is a systemd unit (`--cluster-cidr=10.244.0.0/16 --proxy-mode=ipvs --ipvs-scheduler=rr --kubeconfig=/etc/kubernetes/kube-proxy.conf --master=https://192.168.160.1:6443`), 3 masters behind a HAProxy on 192.168.160.1:6443. The asker quotes a 2016 comment ("add static routes for the service CIDR") and says the kube-proxy debugging steps show it cannot reach the master. One answer (mdaniel, 1 vote): do not run kube-proxy under systemd (it is meant to be started by kubelet), the `:443` is the `kubernetes.default` Service port, and put the master address into `kube-proxy.conf`. Comments: the asker had everything as command-line flags and has to "work out how to add the master information to the kubeconfig"; later "I have got this working" (what was changed is not said), then a new error (`kubelet does not have ClusterDNS IP configured`).
