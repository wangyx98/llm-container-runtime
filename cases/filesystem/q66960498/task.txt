On this shared Ubuntu machine a small Kubernetes-like node is modelled on the question "Purge Kubernetes Image Cache on containerd runtime with daemonSet": the asker wants to purge images from the node's cache on a cadence from a DaemonSet that mounts containerd's socket; in that container `ctr image ls` and `nerdctl` show nothing, and no error (the thread's answer: the tools use containerd's default namespace, Kubernetes uses another one, and crictl is bound to it). What follows is an ADAPTATION: no Kubernetes; the CRI is called with crictl, as a kubelet would, on a private containerd 1.6.21 with runc 1.1.12, offline; the "DaemonSet" is a CRI Pod, purger, whose container has the node's containerd socket mounted at /run/containerd/containerd.sock (a hostPath of type Socket) and carries /ctr and /crictl.

The node lives below /var/lib/bench66960498 and /run/bench66960498; `sudo /var/lib/bench66960498/bin/nodectl status` shows it. Two wrappers talk to this node and to no other: `sudo /var/lib/bench66960498/bin/ctr ...` (namespace k8s.io) and `sudo /var/lib/bench66960498/bin/crictl ...`. Three Pods run, each with one container "main": web (image bench66960498.local/app/web:1), jobs (bench66960498.local/scratch/busy:1) and purger (bench66960498.local/tools:1). To look into the purger's container: `sudo /var/lib/bench66960498/bin/crictl exec <container id> /ctr -a /run/containerd/containerd.sock images ls -q`; its /crictl needs `-r unix:///run/containerd/containerd.sock`. The node's images: app/web:1, scratch/busy:1, scratch/old:1, scratch/old:2, keep/data:1, tools:1 and pause:1 (the sandbox image), all below bench66960498.local/.

Your task: write the job that purges, as the DaemonSet would run it on a cadence: the executable script /var/lib/bench66960498/job/purge-images.sh (run by root, no arguments). The scope is the repositories below bench66960498.local/scratch/: it removes the images of the scope that no container of the node uses, completely (from the CRI and from containerd's k8s.io namespace, with their ids, nothing left untagged), and leaves every other image alone: the one of the scope that a container uses (scratch/busy), the ones outside the scope (keep/data, tools, app/web) and the sandbox image. The work must go through the purger's container (the script may use the node's crictl only to find it and to look at the node: ps, pods, inspect, exec; not to list or remove images, and not the node's ctr); the Pods and their containers stay as they are; the node's containerd is not restarted or reconfigured. Running the job again, also when there is nothing to purge, is harmless and exits 0; it will be run again with new unused images on the node. Run the job once now.

The current user has passwordless sudo. Unless stated otherwise, assume container-runtime and system commands need sudo.

Return the commands needed to complete the task.
