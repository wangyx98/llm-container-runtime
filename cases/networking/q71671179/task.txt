The problem: the master pulls and runs registry.bench71671179.test:35751/iam-config-server:0.0.2 without any problem, but on both workers the pull fails (ImagePullBackOff in the Kubernetes version of the story) with "pinging container registry registry.bench71671179.test:35751: ... x509: certificate signed by unknown authority", although the secret with the credentials is in place and the certificate was copied somewhere on the workers.
