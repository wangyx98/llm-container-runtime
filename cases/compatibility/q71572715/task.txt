This machine has a containerd that was installed and is run on its own (no Docker
daemon, no CRI-O): its socket is /run/bench71572715/containerd.sock (owned by root);
use it with `sudo ctr -a /run/bench71572715/containerd.sock ...`. It is started and
stopped with /var/lib/bench71572715/bin/containerdctl (start, stop, restart, status). An
independent workload, the container bench71572715-workload (namespace default), runs
in it and has nothing to do with Kubernetes.

k3s also runs on this machine, as a single node called bench71572715-node. It is
configured in /etc/rancher/k3s/config.yaml (the options of the k3s command line, written
without the leading dashes) and is started and stopped with /var/lib/bench71572715/bin/k3sctl
(start, stop, restart, status). As installed by default, k3s starts its own embedded
containerd, a child process of k3s that dies with it:

    $ sudo /var/lib/bench71572715/bin/k3sctl stop
    $ pgrep -x containerd         # the containerd of k3s is gone with it
    $ sudo ctr -a /run/bench71572715/containerd.sock version     # the other one is untouched

Today the node runs on that embedded containerd, so the lifecycle of the runtime is tied
to the one of k3s: stopping, restarting or upgrading k3s takes the runtime down with it.
The engineer wants the two decoupled.

Your task: make k3s use the containerd that is already installed
(/run/bench71572715/containerd.sock) as its container runtime, instead of starting its
own, and leave k3s running with that configuration. Afterwards, stopping k3s must leave
that containerd process, its RPC and the workload running.

Requirements:

1. At the end k3s must be running (started by k3sctl, as it is now), and its node
   bench71572715-node must be Ready and report the pre-installed containerd as its
   runtime. k3s may need up to about a minute to come up and for the node to update;
   the check waits up to 2 minutes.
2. k3s must not start a containerd of its own.
3. Do not restart, stop, replace or reconfigure the pre-installed containerd, and do not
   touch the workload bench71572715-workload: the check requires them to be the same
   processes as before, still running.
4. The check will stop k3s itself with `k3sctl stop` and then look at that containerd,
   at its workload and at a new container started in it. Do not stop k3s at the end of
   your script.
5. Do not install, download or replace programs, and do not change the k3s binary.
6. The script runs without a terminal: stdin is not a TTY, nothing can be typed.
7. The current user has passwordless sudo. Unless stated otherwise, assume
   container-runtime and system commands need `sudo`.
8. Your script must finish by itself: no interactive prompts, no command that runs in
   the foreground until it is interrupted, and no command that waits forever.

Return the commands needed to complete the task.
